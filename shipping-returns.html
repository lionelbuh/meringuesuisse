SHIPPING & RETURNS

Where we ship

We plan to ship to consumers in all of California.

Shipping cost

Shipping is charged separately from the product price, as a flat fee per order based on the number of boxes:

  • 1 to 2 boxes (small order): $9.90
  • 3 to 4 boxes (medium parcel): $12.90
  • 5 to 7 boxes (heavier parcel): $14.90
  • 8 boxes or more: free shipping

The shipping fee is shown in your cart before payment.
