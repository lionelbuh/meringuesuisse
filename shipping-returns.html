SHIPPING & RETURNS

Where we deliver

We ship to addresses in California with USPS Ground Advantage.

Free local delivery in the Temecula area: if you live in ZIP code 92590, 92591, 92592, 92593 (Temecula), 92562, 92563, 92564 (Murrieta), 92595 (Wildomar) or 92596 (French Valley), we deliver your order ourselves and leave it at your front door, free of charge. Choose "Free local delivery" in your cart and enter your ZIP code.

Shipping cost

Shipping is charged separately from the product price, as a flat fee per order based on the number of boxes:

  • 1 to 2 boxes (small order): $9.90
  • 3 to 4 boxes (medium parcel): $12.90
  • 5 to 7 boxes (heavier parcel): $14.90
  • 8 boxes or more: free shipping
  • Local delivery in the Temecula area: free

The shipping fee is shown in your cart before payment.

Preparation & delivery times

Every order is made for you. Because of high demand, please allow 3 to 5 business days to prepare your meringues.

We ship Monday to Wednesday only, so your box never waits in a warehouse over the weekend. USPS Ground Advantage usually delivers in 2 to 5 business days, so most orders arrive within 5 to 10 business days of ordering.

Local deliveries in the Temecula area are left at your front door once your order is ready, usually within 3 to 5 business days.

Storage

Keep your meringues in their original box, in a cool, dry place away from humidity and direct sunlight. No need to refrigerate.

For the best crispness and flavor, enjoy them within 10 days of purchase.

Although, if you can resist them for that long... 😉

Ordering

Our cottage food permit from Riverside County is pending, so we are not taking orders yet. Join the waiting list to be the first to know when ordering opens.Orders are placed and paid securely online.

Returns

Because our meringues are food, we cannot accept returns.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, please contact us within 3 days of delivery with a photo of the box. We'll send you a replacement or refund your purchase to your original payment method. There's no need to send anything back.
